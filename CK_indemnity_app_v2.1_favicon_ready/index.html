About Constantly Kiting

Constantly Kiting is one of South Africa’s leading kitesurfing schools, based in Langebaan. Established in 2007, the school has built a strong reputation for professional instruction, safety, and unforgettable experiences on the water.

The school is owned and operated by Wesley Fourie, a passionate kitesurfer and experienced instructor dedicated to growing the sport and delivering world-class training to both beginners and advanced riders.

Over the years, Constantly Kiting has trained thousands of students from around the world, offering lessons, rentals, and advanced coaching in one of the best kitesurfing locations globally.
